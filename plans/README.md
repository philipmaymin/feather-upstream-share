## Live audit baseline

- 14 Rooms, 143 Room-listed chats, no duplicate Room membership.
- All four durable Leader pointers matched the live Room response; ten legacy/undesignated Rooms correctly had no pointer.
- All 300 indexed chats returned HTTP 200 and at least one message.
- Four stale non-controller Room assignments resolved to no session and empty-success transcripts.
- `#films` alone ships 94 historical chats in every unpaginated Rooms payload.
- `/api/search` ignored current OMP content, took about 11 seconds, and blocked `/api/health` for the same interval.
- `/api/shell` upgraded then closed 1011.
- Healthy controls: cross-chat selection generation guards, main transcript SSE replay/dedupe, Room expansion/opening, immutable release hashing/rollback, and current Leader-pointer consistency.

## Deferred findings not yet planned

These are real, vetted findings but lower leverage than the five plans above or too risky to combine casually:

- Sidecar delivery reports success before recipient delivery and has no durable retry state.
- Sidecar full snapshot plus SSE replay can duplicate messages on initial load/reconnect.
- Promotion health certifies timestamp only, not instance ID/commit/tree/asset; the behavioral precheck is optional.
- Writable instances have no exclusive lease for shared Room/tmux controller state.
- Chat send can persist success even if the exact marker remains visibly unsent after Enter attempts.
- Terminal reconnect has no output sequence/resume checkpoint.
- Mobile Room controls and resident rows use undersized/non-semantic hit targets.

## Findings considered and rejected

- Null Leader on the ten undesignated legacy Rooms is not by itself corruption; only four durable Leader pointers exist, and all four were returned correctly.
- Pulse controller sessions are intentionally hidden from human chat lists; their lack of operational inspectability is a status/observability issue, not lost chat history.
- Room grouping itself correctly gives explicit assignment precedence and deduplicates IDs across Rooms.
- Main chat selection/SSE isolation is strong and was not generalized from the separate Sidecar replay defect.
- Current immutable asset serving was coherent; the gap is promotion certification, not an observed stale asset in this audit.
